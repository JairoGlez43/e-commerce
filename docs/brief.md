# Project brief

## The idea
A website for a local shop in Cuba that sells mostly food products. Today the owner sells
from a fixed stall and posts products in WhatsApp groups. The first goal is a **demo** to
show the owner, so they can see the value and decide whether to buy the site.

## Who it is for and what problem it solves
- **The owner:** has to post stock updates in WhatsApp groups one by one. Old messages get
  buried and lost, and the groups are so crowded that customers may never see the posts.
- **Customers:** local buyers who want to know what is available, at what price, at any time.
- **What the web adds:** one always-current place for stock, prices and product details,
  and a single link to share instead of many messages.

## Requirements (v1)
- Show the products in stock, with prices.
- Show each product's details.
- The site must be as lightweight as possible (see rules).
